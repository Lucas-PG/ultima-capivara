PROJECT
"Ultima Capivara" is a stylized multiplayer FPS (target finish: Overwatch 2 / Fortnite / Valorant, commercial quality) on a tropical Brazilian island where the players are capybaras. Stack: Three.js 0.186, TypeScript, Vite 8, host-authoritative simulation in a Web Worker, PeerJS multiplayer, Vitest, Playwright with real Chrome (channel "chrome", ANGLE Metal), Blender 5.0.1 headless. Read docs/overhaul/autonomous-progress.md for context, then docs/overhaul/guns-short-report.md, guns-long-report.md and char-polish-report.md. You have full creative freedom inside your scope: research, experiment, be creative, and reject your own mediocre results. Everything should be as high quality as you can make it.

ROLE
You own the first-person arms. The third-person character was redesigned (round 2 merged, a round 3 of the head, fur and knees runs in parallel), and the first-person arms no longer match it: they still use the old paw sculpt (tools/blender/paw_sculpt.py) and the old darker fur. Make the arms you see in first person unmistakably the same capybara players see in third person, then re-fit every weapon to them. How you get there is your call.

WHAT MATCHING MEANS
- The paw: the character now has its own big leathery paw (tools/blender/capy_hand.py, four thick digits with short blunt claws, a lower abducted outer digit that works as a thumb, a padded palm, bare dark leathery skin on the palm and digits, fur on the back up to the knuckles). Its paw space already follows the first-person rig convention (wrist at the origin, digits along +y, back of the paw +z, index toward -x on the right paw; digit names index, middle, ring, thumb), so the same finger curls can drive it. Build the first-person paw from it.
- Scale: third-person guns are drawn at 1.3 times their size (TP_WEAPON_SCALE in src/render/capybara.ts) so the bigger paw holds them; first-person guns are at 1.0. Keep the paw-to-gun proportion the same in both views (for example, the first-person paw at about 1/1.3 of the third-person paw), unless you find a better solution that reads right from the eye.
- The forearm, fur, paw skin, claws and rolled linen cuff match the character's (colours and lock sizes in the round 2 section of docs/overhaul/char-polish-report.md; the round 3 agent may refine the fur look: see coordination below). Fur shells on the forearm and the back of the paw as now, tuned to read like the third-person forearm.
- A board that puts the first-person forearm and paw next to the third-person forearm and paw at similar framing and light is part of the evidence.

WEAPONS
All nine weapons (pistol, revolver, SMG, M4, Doze shotgun, Carabina DMR, sniper, Lanca-coco, facao) must be re-fitted to the new paws in every state: hip, aimed, sprint, fire, draw and holster, inspect, and every reload (empty and tactical magazine swaps, revolver crane, ejector and speedloader, shell-by-shell shotgun, bolt cycle, coco hopper, machete swings and chop).
- Anatomy rules (they were hard won; do not regress them): the support paw wraps the handguard or fore-end from the lower left near the base of the barrel, palm against the side, thumb along the upper edge pointing forward, digits forward and curling under (tips 250 to 340 degrees around the bore); never the flat fingers-up side grip; the firing paw shows its back to the eye at the hip with the index on the trigger and the other digits wrapped round the grip; the pistol and revolver are held two-handed with the support paw cupping the firing paw from below-left. Reload contacts follow the moving part (magazine, slide, bolt, pump, cylinder, loader, shells, hopper).
- Tooling: tools/qa/grip-probe.mjs (per digit signed distance to the gun and digit angles around the bore), tools/qa/grip-fit.mjs (pattern-search solver over wrist, frame, curls and thumb spread), tools/qa/vmall.mjs, vmtune.mjs, vmmotion.mjs, capture.mjs (fp-*, ads-* poses). Runtime: src/render/fp-arms.ts (ArmsRig, IK, finger hinges and spread from the bind geometry, furShellMesh), src/render/viewmodel-specs.ts (grips per weapon), viewmodel-choreo.ts, viewmodel-anims.ts, src/render/weapons.ts. Accept a fit only at a worst clearance of -0.5 mm or better in every sampled pose, and only after looking at it from the eye and both sides.
- You may adjust a weapon model where the new paw needs it (tools/blender/arsenal.py, rebuilt on the Linux machine, budgets kept: under 25k triangles and 1.3 MB per first-person gun), but prefer re-fitting the paw.

RESEARCH
Research how shipped shooters build and animate first-person arms and weapon handling (published talks, breakdowns, tutorials and guides: first-person rigs, hand IK to weapon sockets, per-weapon hand poses, reload choreography and timing, readable silhouettes at the screen edges, stylized or animal hands in first person) and write the useful findings into docs/overhaul/fp-arms-research.md with sources. Codex CLI may generate reference images (codex exec --skip-git-repo-check -m gpt-6.1-sol, asking its image generation tool to save a PNG at an exact path); the boards in docs/art/references/ are guides, not anatomy truth.
