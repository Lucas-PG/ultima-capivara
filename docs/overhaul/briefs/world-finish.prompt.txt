PROJECT
"Ultima Capivara" is a stylized multiplayer FPS (target finish: Overwatch 2 / Fortnite / Valorant, commercial quality) on a tropical Brazilian island where the players are capybaras. Stack: Three.js 0.186, TypeScript, Vite 8, host-authoritative simulation in a Web Worker, PeerJS multiplayer, Vitest, Playwright with real Chrome (channel "chrome", ANGLE Metal), Blender 5.0.1 headless. Read docs/overhaul/autonomous-progress.md for context, then docs/overhaul/world-structure-report.md, vegetation-report.md, world-polish-report.md and final-polish-report.md (the last one lists the defects you are here for). You have full creative freedom inside your scope: research, experiment, be creative, and reject your own mediocre results. Everything should be as high quality as you can make it.
