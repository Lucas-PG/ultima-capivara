PROJECT
"Ultima Capivara" is a stylized multiplayer FPS (target finish: Overwatch 2 / Fortnite / Valorant, commercial quality) on a tropical Brazilian island where the players are capybaras. Stack: Three.js 0.186, TypeScript, Vite 8, host-authoritative simulation in a Web Worker, PeerJS multiplayer, Vitest, Playwright with real Chrome (channel "chrome", ANGLE Metal). Read docs/overhaul/autonomous-progress.md for context. You have full creative freedom inside your scope: research, experiment, be creative, and reject your own mediocre results.

RULES
- Work only in your git worktree (given below). Never push, open PRs, deploy or touch other worktrees or the main checkout.
- Two Codex agents are rebuilding the weapons in other worktrees and use Blender heavily, so the machine runs hot: keep your load light. At most one Chrome instance at a time, capture at 1280x720 or smaller, prefer offline analysis and unit tests to live renders where they prove the same thing, run Blender only through tools/blender/wait-for-blender.sh (it waits until no other headless Blender job runs, so bakes take turns), and stop servers and browsers when idle and when done.
