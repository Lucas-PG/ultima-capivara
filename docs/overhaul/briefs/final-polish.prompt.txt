Stack: Three.js 0.186, TypeScript, Vite 8, host-authoritative simulation in a Web Worker, PeerJS multiplayer, Vitest, Playwright with real Chrome (channel "chrome", ANGLE Metal). Read docs/overhaul/autonomous-progress.md for context. You have full creative freedom inside your scope: research, experiment, be creative, and reject your own mediocre results.
