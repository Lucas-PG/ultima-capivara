PROJECT
"Ultima Capivara" is a stylized multiplayer FPS (target finish: Overwatch 2 / Fortnite / Valorant, commercial quality) on a tropical Brazilian island where the players are capybaras. Stack: Three.js 0.186, TypeScript, Vite 8, host-authoritative simulation in a Web Worker, PeerJS multiplayer, Vitest, Playwright with real Chrome (channel "chrome", ANGLE Metal). Read docs/overhaul/autonomous-progress.md for context. You have full creative freedom inside your scope: research, experiment, be creative, and reject your own mediocre results.

SCOPE (own all of it; find the problems yourself, the lists below are starting points)
1. Gunfeel and combat readability: time to kill and damage model per weapon, headshots, spread and bloom, recoil patterns and recovery, ADS speed and movement penalties, fire cadence, hit registration feel (hitstop or micro freeze if it fits, camera kick, damage flinch, FOV punch), low-health state, kill confirmation moments, feedback timing with the new audio (src/sound/*, merged: use its hit, kill and near-miss hooks). Balance every weapon for fun and fairness; write the numbers and reasoning down.
2. Movement and controls ("keys and functionalities"): input responsiveness, sprint, crouch, jump and landing, consider a slide, lean or mantle only if it makes combat better and fits the game, weapon swap speed, reload interruption rules, melee, interact and pickup flow, emotes, default key bindings and a rebinding data model (the UI agent builds the settings screen that edits it: keep a small clean interface), mouse sensitivity and ADS sensitivity, toggle or hold options.
3. VFX to a commercial stylized standard (reference docs/art/references/vfx-sheet.jpg and hud-vfx-research.md): muzzle flashes per weapon class, tracers, impacts per material (stone, wood, metal, sand, water, foliage, capybaras), decals, shell ejection, smoke and dust, the coconut explosion and its shockwave, water splashes and wakes, the storm edge, the parachute and plane, pickups and chests, healing and armour effects. Pool everything and stay inside the perf budget (about 260 draws and 1.8M triangles at medium in busy fights); scale by the Low, Medium and High presets.
4. Research how Call of Duty, Valorant, Apex Legends, Overwatch 2 and Fortnite make combat feel good (feedback layering, recoil design, TTK, hit feedback, VFX readability versus clutter) and write docs/overhaul/combat-research.md with sources.
5. Verify in live matches with bots (tools/qa/play.mjs) and with frame sequences, not stills only; keep an eye on fairness in multiplayer (the simulation is host-authoritative in src/simulation).
