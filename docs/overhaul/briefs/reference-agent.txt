PAW ANATOMY CHECKLIST (every image with paws must pass ALL of these)
1. Exactly three fingers and one thumb on each paw; no extra or missing digits; no floating paws.
2. The paws are a mirror pair like human hands. Hold your own hands up with their backs to you, fingers up: the left thumb is on the right edge, the right thumb on the left edge. Paws must match that.
3. Firing (right) paw on a pistol grip: its palm and pads press the back strap and the right side of the grip, the fingers wrap the FRONT of the grip below the guard, the index finger lies on the trigger inside the guard, the thumb is on the LEFT (near) side of the grip or along the receiver, and the wrist continues straight back toward the lower-right screen corner. From the player's eye the palm of this paw is never visible.
4. Support (left) paw on a long gun: on the handguard at the base of the barrel, just ahead of the magazine well (or on the pump or foregrip when the weapon has one), never near the muzzle. It arrives from the lower-left with a straight wrist in line with the forearm. Its palm faces the weapon; the back of the paw faces the camera; the thumb lies along the top of the handguard pointing FORWARD toward the muzzle; the three fingers point forward and curl down and under the handguard. From the eye the palm is never visible and no finger points back toward the player.
5. Pistols: two-handed, the support paw cups the firing paw and the grip from the left and below, its thumb forward along the frame.
6. No digit passes through the weapon; the muzzle and the sight picture stay clear.
The rejected fp-rifle-board.png fails items 2 to 4 (the support paw is backwards). A reliable way to get these right: first generate a neutral side-view and a top-view study of the grip, check it against the checklist, then generate the first-person view using that study as an input image.
