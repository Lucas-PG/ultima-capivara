ROLE AND WORKTREE
You are the audio director and sound designer. Worktree: /Users/lucas_gaspe/dev/ultima-capivara/.claude/worktrees/audio (branch audio-pass). Dev server port 5175. Report: docs/overhaul/audio-report.md.

WHAT THE USER SAID (verbatim essence, top priority)
1. "On the island, there is this weird kind of white noise sound that's always on, which is very annoying. It's like a background noise that's just always on when you're playing."
2. "Gun firing sounds like footsteps, so you can never tell what's going on by sound. Let's make sure to be extra careful about that."

GOALS
1. Find the always-on noise and prove its source: instrument or render the game's audio graph (for example with an OfflineAudioContext through the QA hooks, or by capturing the master output in Chrome) and measure the idle island mix (spectrum, level, modulation). Fix it at the source. Ambience must be designed, not a constant hiss: layered, band-limited, spatial and modulated beds (surf by the shore, wind on heights, birds and insects in the trees, harbour and town life), quiet under combat.
2. Make combat readable by ear. Every weapon gets a distinct, punchy signature (transient crack, body and tail, plus its mechanics: slide, bolt, pump, cylinder, magazine), with distance cues (far shots darker, with a delayed tail or echo). Footsteps are material-based (stone, wood, sand, grass, shallow water), shorter, quieter and clearly directional, and enemy footsteps stay readable for gameplay while your own are subtler. Gunfire and footsteps must be unmistakably different in loudness, spectrum and envelope.
3. Mix and feedback: a loudness hierarchy (your own and enemy gunfire, hits and kills, reloads, footsteps, ambience), ambience ducking under combat, a master limiter so nothing clips, distinct hit, headshot and kill confirmation sounds, UI sounds consistent with the painted style, and positional audio that tells direction and distance.
4. Research how Call of Duty, Valorant, Apex Legends, Overwatch 2 and Battlefield design weapon, footstep and ambience audio for readability (frequency separation, loudness hierarchy, distance cues, occlusion) and write docs/overhaul/audio-research.md with sources.
5. Tests that protect the intent: measure rendered sounds (loudness, spectral centroid, envelope) and fail if gunfire and footsteps overlap too much, if the idle ambience contains a constant broadband noise floor above a threshold you justify, or if the master can clip.
Stay inside a sensible audio budget (voices, pooling, decoding cost on Low).
