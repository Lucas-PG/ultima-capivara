PROJECT
"Ultima Capivara" is a stylized multiplayer FPS (target finish: Overwatch 2 / Fortnite / Valorant, commercial quality) on a tropical Brazilian island where the players are capybaras. Stack: Three.js 0.186, TypeScript, Vite 8, host-authoritative simulation in a Web Worker, PeerJS multiplayer, Vitest, Playwright with real Chrome (channel "chrome", ANGLE Metal). Read docs/overhaul/autonomous-progress.md for context. You have full creative freedom inside your scope: research, experiment, be creative, and reject your own mediocre results.

WHAT THE USER SAID (verbatim essence)
"When you die, the spectating camera is very bugged. It's very hard to spectate after you die, which is going to be slightly annoying once people are playing with real friends."

GOALS
1. Death and spectator camera: reproduce every case first (right after death, cycling targets forward and back, the target dies or leaves, deaths in water, falls and the storm, last player standing, the match ending, respawn in modes that respawn), then fix each one with a test: smooth follow without clipping into walls, a clear target and switch prompt in the HUD (name, health, weapon), stable input, no snapping. Consider a short kill cam if it fits. Verify with at least two real clients over the local multiplayer harness.
2. HUD coherence and polish toward docs/art/references/hud-mockup.jpg (read docs/art/references/hud-vfx-research.md): compact, readable at a glance, never covering the centre, one visual language (the painted golden-hour style with a Brazilian tropical identity) for health and armour, ammo and reserve, the weapon strip, compass or minimap, kill feed, hit markers and kill confirmation, damage direction, storm and objective prompts, and the onboarding card. Check it at 16:9 and ultrawide and at small sizes.
3. Menus and flows: main menu, play and lobby, settings, pause, results and rematch, loading screen. Make them feel like a finished commercial game. You may generate key art, loading screens and backgrounds with Codex image generation (codex exec --skip-git-repo-check -m gpt-6.1-sol); optimise runtime images (WebP or AVIF, sensible sizes) and keep the download budget in mind.
4. Research how Valorant, Apex Legends, Overwatch 2, Fortnite and Call of Duty design their HUD, kill feed, spectator and death flows, and record findings with sources in docs/overhaul/ui-research.md.
