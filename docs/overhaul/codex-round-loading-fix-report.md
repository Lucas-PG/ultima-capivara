# First round waits for the whole room

2 October 2026. Follow-up to the slow-guest failure in `round-modes.e2e.spec.ts`. Source commits: `3fd7354` and `15f9bb6`, based on `8111d38`.

The first buy clock previously waited only for the host's scene preparation. The host also acknowledged before its first game frame and HUD update. A guest could therefore finish loading with only 5 to 8.7 seconds of the 12-second buy window remaining.

Round matches now wait for every currently connected human to prepare the island, its assets and avatars, draw its first game frame, update the HUD and finish removing the loading overlay. Only then does that client acknowledge the current match. The host derives the guest ID from the live reliable connection; a packet cannot acknowledge another player. Stale match IDs and replaced connections are ignored. Generated bots need no loading acknowledgement.

The worker holds the opening countdown and simulation time at zero while continuing fresh transport ticks and snapshots. This keeps newly arrived players and connection status visible without accumulating movement, shots, countdown or buy-time debt. Once the wait ends, the existing three-second opening countdown and the complete preparation/buy phase run normally. Duelo still prepares for three seconds; team buying still lasts 12 seconds. Subsequent rounds have no new wait. Royale, Correria and Corrente keep their existing startup timing.

The wait has a single **30,000 ms wall-clock cap**, beginning immediately before the worker's initial snapshot. Thirty seconds allows substantial cold asset/shader preparation while matching the existing connection-recovery grace period. One stuck client cannot hold the room indefinitely. Joins, reconnects, repeated acknowledgements and mismatched acknowledgements cannot renew the deadline. At the cap, the ready players proceed through the normal opening countdown and full buy window; a client still loading may miss that window. The host's worker owns both the deadline and game clock.

Disconnecting or expiring removes that human from the wait. A reconnect before release requires a new acknowledgement. A same-match `start` resets the guest's local preparation and acknowledgement while reusing its existing renderer. A human arriving before release is included in the remaining wait; an arrival after release cannot reopen it. Fresh round-mode arrivals consistently follow the existing late-join rule: replace the bot/seat, watch until the next round, and receive the correct round kit then. Their spectator metadata contains only free pistol/facão basics, never the generic arena SMG, and buying is rejected while dead. Reconnecting an existing human preserves its body, equipment and funds. Stop, leave and rematch clear readiness and create a fresh deadline for the new match.

Protocol **14** adds the reliable, match-bound `match-loaded` control message. The actor tuple and round snapshot shape are unchanged. Host and guests must reload the same build; protocol 13 peers are rejected by the existing version gate. A small QA-only worker notification records whether startup released by readiness or by the cap and its elapsed wait. It is excluded from normal production builds and adds no player setting or HUD layout change.

Real worker tests cover held time and fresh snapshots, no body/kit/shot drift, a complete first buy window, the exact finite cap, disconnected blockers, current late-arrival metadata, stale rematch acknowledgements, teardown, later-round timing and all three legacy startup modes. Transport tests cover connection identity, stale packets and same-match reconnect starts. Round tests preserve reconnect body/funds and reject purchases from fresh spectators.

The original `>=10` seconds assertion remains. The delayed case confirms host time stays exactly zero during at least eight seconds of deliberately withheld renderer loading; the release-reason assertion requires both clients' readiness rather than a timeout. Purchases, refunds, team spectating, shared score and round two remain checked.
