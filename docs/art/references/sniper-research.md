# Sniper first-person research
Updated 29 September 2026. [Shared evidence](animation-research.md). [Image QA](README.md).

## Design and source conflict
Long fluted barrel, muzzle brake, folded bipod, wood thumbhole stock with teal leaves, black/brass scope and detachable box magazine. Source views give inconsistent-looking bolt-side detail and a rear rod that generation repeatedly turns into a second knob. Establish ONE conventional right-side turnbolt, ONE angled operating stem and ONE spherical knob. Right paw operates it; do not model two bolt handles. This explicit mechanical choice outranks contradictory generated views.

Class candidates: MW2019 AX-50, MWII SP-X 80, MWIII KATT-AMR, BF2042 SWS-10, Valorant Operator, Apex Sentinel, Overwatch Widowmaker, Fortnite Ballistic Reaper Sniper. Widowmaker transforms a different weapon and has no corresponding manual bolt cycle; borrow scoped framing only. Apex Charge Rifle is also not a bolt-action reload template. [Infinity Ward scope/animation discussion](https://blog.activision.com/call-of-duty/2019-07/Modern-Warfare-Initial-Intel-Detailing-Advancements-in-Animation-and-Authenticity), [Riot arsenal](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/arsenal/), [Ballistic weapon classes](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-goes-first-person-with-ballistic-a-tactical-5v5-shooter-launching-in-early-access-dec-11). Exact key times below are proposals, not measurements.

## Hip, ADS, sprint and inspect
Hip fore-end recedes into scene, receiver/scope low-right. Left paw grips rear WOOD fore-end ahead of magazine, never bipod or bare barrel. Right thumb fits thumbhole; index in guard and only two other fingers below. ADS centers clear scope glass and thin internal crosshair, magnifies same scene inside glass only, shows both lower forearms. One rear cap, front cap farther along the axis, no duplicated side-by-side cap ears.

Sprint .25 s dip into two-paw close carry; .35 s recovery. Scope mass gives delayed settle, camera stable. Inspect 0-.40 lift, .40-1.20 wood/scope side, 1.20-1.85 opposite face, 1.85-2.40 return. No bolt stroke or magazine removal on cosmetic inspect.

## Tactical reload, chamber loaded, 2.45 s
.00-.25 present well with bolt CLOSED; .25-.70 left withdraws partial box magazine; .70-1.25 stow and retrieve fresh; 1.25-1.70 align/seat; 1.70-2.05 restore fore-end grip; 2.05-2.45 settle. Right stays on thumbhole grip, index clear. No bolt cycle and no live-round ejection.

## Empty reload, 3.35 s, six board keys
1. .00-.50: left supports fore-end and stock remains shouldered; right leaves grip, lifts bolt then draws it fully rearward. Sample .45.
2. .50-1.05: right returns to stock grip; left extracts empty magazine completely. Sample 1.00, empty well.
3. 1.05-1.65: left retrieves fresh magazine and aligns below well with gap. Sample 1.60.
4. 1.65-2.15: seat fully with left heel at base. Sample 2.10.
5. 2.15-2.90: left restores fore-end support before right leaves grip; right pushes bolt forward and turns down to lock. Sample 2.80.
6. 2.90-3.35: right returns to grip, index can return to trigger; settle original hip.

Two paws total at every instant. No third paw holding grip while right operates bolt. A rearward bolt is visible through receiver opening, not a duplicate metal rod. Box magazine fully absent in keys 2/3, fully installed in 4-6.

## Weight and known limits
Right bolt lift has rotational resistance then a rear stop. Magazine seating is upward. Forward bolt stroke has resistance followed by a short downward locking beat. Left paw and shoulder carry weight whenever right leaves grip. Keep scope steady relative receiver. The generated reload still duplicates bolt components and does not reliably establish every empty well; it is explicitly failed mechanical QA. Follow this timeline and resolve mechanism in neutral modelling before animation.
