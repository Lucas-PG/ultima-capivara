# First-person animation research and evidence
Updated 29 September 2026.

## Evidence boundary
Publisher pages were read in this pass. Linked footage in older notes was located, not frame-counted, and must not be presented as watched or timed. The pose sequences below are original animation direction for Ultima Capivara. Exact contact trajectories, angle values and key times are proposed, except the local M4 2.5-second benchmark constraint and explicitly dated publisher timing examples. These boards are generated concept art, not mechanically verified 3D poses. See README.md for image-specific failures.

## Concrete findings and transfer
- Modern Warfare 2019: Infinity Ward describes separate tactical and empty reloads, physical magazine transitions, class-dependent heft, active idle and ADS reloads. Transfer continuous hand/object contact, distinct empty-state action and small weight responses. Do not add a charging action to every partial reload. [Infinity Ward animation interview, 2 July 2019](https://blog.activision.com/call-of-duty/2019-07/Modern-Warfare-Initial-Intel-Detailing-Advancements-in-Animation-and-Authenticity).
- Modern Warfare II (2022): weapon platforms and attachment choices change silhouettes. Freeze a base weapon and attachment set before comparing hip and ADS. The publisher launch material does not give a per-weapon pose timeline. [Multiplayer and Gunsmith overview](https://www.callofduty.com/blog/2022/09/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-II-next-multiplayer-maps-modes-gunsmith). The previously located [reload and inspect compilation](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=xBj_rqzGZdk) remains a footage lead, not a measured source.
- Modern Warfare III (2023): partial reload cancellation and longer tactical sprint are explicit features. Tac-Stance is a separate canted aiming state between hip and ADS. Do not mistake its cant for an ADS alignment error or copy its cancel rules into this game without a gameplay decision. [Worldwide reveal](https://www.callofduty.com/uk/en/blog/2023/08/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-III-worldwide-full-reveal-announcement), [movement guide](https://www.callofduty.com/guides/training/call-of-duty-modern-warfare-III-play-guides-multiplayer-movement).
- Battlefield 2042: its display/FOV controls separate peripheral framing from sight presentation. Attachment and update version matter. Use M5A3, MP9, MCS-880, DM7, SWS-10, G57 and M44 as class-specific comparison candidates. Do not claim a universal inspect clip. [PC display settings](https://www.ea.com/able/resources/battlefield-2042/pc/display), [Update 5.0](https://www.ea.com/games/battlefield/news/battlefield-2042-update-notes-5-0). [Reload compilation lead](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=T_wZ4mRj3-Q) was not frame-measured here.
- Valorant: Riot describes intentional, practiced hand motion, recognizable two/three-beat animation and audio patterns, unique silhouettes, and minimal ADS hardware to avoid covering the view. Transfer distinct contact beats and clean negative space. Its rifle holographic presentation is not an iron-notch template. [Arsenal art and animation process](https://playvalorant.com/en-gb/news/dev/how-the-valorant-arsenal-was-built/). [Official arsenal](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/arsenal/) identifies Bucky as pump action, Sheriff as a six-shooter, and separates pistols, SMGs, rifles, snipers, heavies and melee. Running/walking must not be renamed a CoD tactical-sprint reference.
- Apex Legends: Respawn changed Flatline sight geometry because side fins obscured targets and skin sight differences affected fairness. Transfer a stable open target lane and equal sight usability across cosmetic variants. [Iron-sight update, 16 September 2022](https://www.ea.com/games/apex-legends/apex-legends/news/iron-sight-update). Published Mastiff timings in the January 2024 patch are 0.9 s tactical and 1.6 s empty. They describe that game's reload segment, not a full six-shell reload for Doze. [Ignite midseason notes](https://www.ea.com/ea-play-live/news/ignite-mid-season-patch-notes). Charge Rifle's August 2023 reload changed to 3.5/4.6 s tactical/empty, illustrating class/state differences, not a bolt-action model. [Resurrection notes](https://www.ea.com/en-gb/inside-ea/news/resurrection-patch-notes).
- Overwatch 2: ADS and sprint depend on the hero. Ashe's Viper has aimed and unaimed fire; Cassidy's secondary uses remaining rounds and Combat Roll reloads. These are not generic firearm states. Ashe's September 2024 per-bullet reload changed from 0.2 to 0.25 s. Use this only as a dated rhythm comparison, not today's universal value. [Ashe introduction](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/ptr/2018/11/), [September 2024 patch](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2024/09/), [Cassidy abilities](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-gb/heroes/cassidy/?mobile-app=true&theme=false). Several direct hero pages returned 403 in this research pass. No detailed hand trajectory is claimed from those blocked pages.
- Fortnite: use the December 2024 Ballistic presentation for first-person comparisons, not ordinary third-person Battle Royale framing. The launch article shows AR, shotgun, SMG, sniper and pistol families and documents first-person spread/recoil reticle options. Its exact reload contacts were not timed here. [Ballistic launch](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-goes-first-person-with-ballistic-a-tactical-5v5-shooter-launching-in-early-access-dec-11). Epic's first-person device announcement explicitly warned that some ranged weapons lacked custom first-person reloads. Do not treat every Creative weapon as a complete FP reference. [First-person device announcement](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-first-person-now-available-as-an-experimental-feature-in-uefn-and-creative). These are historical references, not claims of current mode availability.

## Shared shot and anatomy specification
Hip: preserve the central half of the screen and horizon. Muzzle projects into the world; avoid a broadside inventory view. Receiver lives low-right and both forearms connect to bottom edges. ADS aligns the actual sight hardware and keeps claws below its line. Scoped boards use the same scene inside and outside the optic; magnification belongs inside the glass. No opaque decorative lens.

Three fingers TOTAL including the index, plus one opposable thumb. Right index touches trigger at ready and is straight outside the guard during manipulation. Only two right fingers remain below the guard. Back of the right paw faces the player; thumb belongs to its left side. Left thumb belongs to its right side when the dorsal paw points upward. Support palm presses the weapon, with dorsal fur facing the player, wrist straight, thumb muzzleward, three fingers down-under. In a neutral left-side view both forearms originate behind the rifle on the stock side. Screen-left entry is specific to first-person projection, not a rule for orthographic side views.

For pistol/revolver, support cups the firing paw and grip from left/below, never grips the barrel. Revolver support stays behind the cylinder front. For vertical foregrip, adapt the wrap to that grip instead of pretending it is a horizontal handguard. For wood stock necks, preserve their actual grip angle. Manipulation poses can reveal support pads when physically justified. No paw passes through the weapon or a moving part.

## Animation direction
Use the right wrist/shoulder as the mass anchor except when the bolt-action right paw explicitly operates the bolt while left paw/shoulder carry it. Give magazine extraction, retrieval, alignment, seating and chamber action separate silhouettes. Keep the camera horizon steadier than weapon motion. Seating is a small weight impulse; bolt closure is faster and smaller. Cuffs and fur lag without hiding contact.

Times are elapsed seconds from action start. Board cells are samples, not equal-duration frames. A loaded chamber remains loaded during tactical magazine exchange. Empty-state chamber action is weapon-specific. Never use a detachable magazine gesture for a tube-fed shotgun, a drum cover opening for a detachable drum, or a firearm reload for a machete. Inspect is cosmetic, cancelable and avoids unnecessary ammunition removal.

## Image limitations
Generated images can invent digits and mechanisms. Notes outrank any failed board. README.md records selected attempts and rejects. Exact timing, viewmodel FOV and camera-space fit need animator blocking and gameplay review, which are outside this art-only task.
