## Acceptance

Every new PNG must be below 4,000,000 bytes. Each paw has three fingers total, including the index, plus one thumb. Paws are a mirror pair. Ready grips hide palm pads from the player, preserve straight wrists, and keep all digits clear of geometry and sights. During reload, the index leaves the trigger and the support paw may rotate to manipulate ammunition. Occlusion does not prove digit count. An ambiguous grip is not a pass.

The M4 benchmark documentation specifies a 2.5-second reload. It takes precedence over the older research proposal of 2.3 seconds tactical and 2.8 seconds empty. Other times are art-direction proposals unless explicitly identified as measured.
