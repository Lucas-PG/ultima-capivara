# Doze shotgun first-person research
Updated 29 September 2026. [Shared evidence](animation-research.md). [Image QA](README.md).

## Design and comparisons
Tube-fed pump shotgun, traditional wood stock neck, ribbed wood sliding fore-end, black receiver/barrel, teal leaves, brass details, coral buttpad, four red shells on LEFT-side carrier. Ejection opening belongs on RIGHT receiver; loading gate underneath ahead of guard. Six-round tube plus chamber is an explicit illustration assumption, not established gameplay capacity.

Compare MW2019 Model 680, MWII Bryson 800, MWIII Lockwood 680, BF2042 MCS-880, Valorant Bucky, Apex Mastiff, Overwatch Ashe's incremental Viper reload and Fortnite Ballistic Hammer Pump. Ashe is a lever rifle and Mastiff a different mechanism, so only transfer insertion rhythm and interrupt readability. Riot identifies Bucky as pump action in its [arsenal](https://playvalorant.com/en-us/arsenal/). Apex's [January 2024 notes](https://www.ea.com/ea-play-live/news/ignite-mid-season-patch-notes) report .9 s tactical/1.6 s empty Mastiff segments, not whole tube fill time. Ashe's [September 2024 patch](https://overwatch.blizzard.com/en-us/news/patch-notes/live/2024/09/) documents .25 s per bullet, also not a shotgun target.

## Hip and ADS
Right paw wraps stock neck at its actual rake; do not add a pistol grip. Left grips rear of moving pump, thumb forward and fingers down-under, well behind muzzle. Center lane open. ADS looks along vent rib to small front bead. Keep bead at center, forearms low, shell carrier consistently on left. Both paws move with pump only when their contact actually belongs to it.

## Sprint and inspect
Sprint .20 s dip into close two-paw carry, .30 s recovery. Inspect 0-.35 lift, .35-1.1 show teal stock/left carrier, 1.1-1.7 right receiver, 1.7-2.2 settle. Avoid unintended pump stroke during inspect, which would alter chamber state.

## Tactical top-up
Chamber loaded and pump forward: .00-.25 roll underside loading gate toward eye; .25-.55 support retrieves one shell; .55-.85 red crimp end enters gate forward along tube axis, brass base rearward; .85-1.00 thumb finishes push; 1.00-1.35 regrip and unwind. Repeat retrieval/insertion at about .60 s per additional shell. Do not pump a live chamber during top-up. The right wrist/shoulder supports weapon while left manipulates.

## Empty reload, six keys
Proposed chamber-first sequence, one chambered shell plus six tube shells. Frame samples are .25, .70, 1.05, 1.45, 4.65, 5.05 s. The initial image prompt used 4.25/4.65 for the last keys before capacity arithmetic was resolved; this written timing supersedes that too-short proposal.

1. .00-.30 pull pump rearward, open right ejection port, roll right side toward eye.
2. .30-.75 place first shell through right port with red crimp toward muzzle and brass base toward stock.
3. .75-1.10 push pump forward, chamber first shell, close action.
4. 1.10-1.50 roll underside toward eye and align first tube shell.
5. 1.50-4.70 complete six tube insertions at roughly .53 s each. Board fifth key samples the last push; the unpictured loop repeats the same contact path.
6. 4.70-5.05 restore left pump grip and ready roll.

Do not substitute the side-saddle for a loading port. Pouch-fed shells leave the carrier count unchanged. Every shell has ONE brass base and ONE red crimp end. The board's shell orientation/base errors remain failed QA, not design choices.

## Impacts and handoff
Rear pump stop gives a short backward mass response; forward closure gives a smaller opposing check. Each shell push gives a tiny receiver response. Keep shoulder and camera stable. Each completed tube insertion is a proposed animation interruption point; gameplay commit behavior is outside this task. References and key times are synthesis, not frame-counted footage.
