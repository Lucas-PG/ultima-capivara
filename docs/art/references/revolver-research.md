# Trinta-e-oito first-person research
Updated 29 September 2026. See [shared evidence](animation-research.md) and the [image ledger](README.md). Times are proposed animation targets, not frame-measured game timings.

## Design and comparison
Preserve the long vent-rib blued barrel, brass botanical engraving, wood grip, blue/white capybara medallion, red front blade and black rear notch. Six chambers, one swing-left cylinder attached by its crane, one ejector rod, no detachable box magazine. The model sheet is authoritative for appearance, while this note establishes the missing state conventions.

MW2019 .357, MWII Basilisk and MWIII TYR are candidate hand-motion comparisons, not measured clips. Do not copy TYR's chamber count into this six-shot design. BF2042 M44 supplies a large sidearm comparison; its detailed reload was not established from the publisher pages. Valorant's official arsenal explicitly calls Sheriff a six-shooter. Apex Wingman is a precision-sidearm silhouette comparison, not a literal swing-cylinder mechanism template. Cassidy's official abilities distinguish ordinary Peacekeeper fire, rapid secondary fire and a Combat Roll that reloads; the roll is not our ordinary reload. Fortnite's documented Ballistic launch arsenal does not establish a first-person revolver sequence, so third-person Battle Royale footage cannot validate these contacts. Publisher evidence and limitations for all seven games are in the shared research.

## Hip, aim, sprint and inspect
Hip: low-right two-paw hold, barrel receding toward scene center. Right palm against backstrap and grip, index on trigger at ready, two other fingers below guard. Left cups grip/firing paw from left and below. Both thumbs stay behind the cylinder front, with no support finger under the barrel. Straight wrists, furry backs to player; exact total three fingers plus thumb each.

ADS: red blade centered in the open black notch, equal gaps and aligned tops. No added ring or optic. Both paw silhouettes remain below the sight line. Keep sight picture centered and background sharp. Avoid borrowing Cassidy or Sheriff secondary fire as ADS.

Sprint proposal: compact two-hand low carry, muzzle angled away from center; 0.18 s entry and 0.25 s return. Do not invent a universal sprint behavior across Valorant and Overwatch heroes.

Inspect proposal, 2.0 s: 0.00-0.35 lift; 0.35-1.00 roll to show medallion and engraving; 1.00-1.55 show opposite flank; 1.55-2.00 return. Keep cylinder closed, no hammer cocking, cylinder flicking or muzzle sweep toward player. Right grip anchors while left supports.

## Reload key poses
Empty speedloader reload, proposed 3.10 s. Read six board cells left-to-right, top-to-bottom.

| Time | State and hand contact |
| --- | --- |
| 0.30 | Right paw holds grip with index straight outside guard. Cylinder released and swung left on attached crane; left supports its rear/outer face. |
| 0.80 | Muzzle slightly elevated. Left presses ejector head; exactly six spent cases leave rearward/downward. No loose cylinder. |
| 1.35 | Right retains grip; left presents a six-round loader coaxial with all six chambers. Visible gap, no cartridge clipping. |
| 1.85 | Six rounds fully seated with bases flush. Empty loader pulled clear to the left; no seventh central round. |
| 2.45 | Left presses cylinder inward into frame, never snaps wrist to throw it closed. Right index remains outside guard. |
| 3.10 | Cylinder locked; left returns to grip cup, both thumbs behind cylinder front; ready sights and trigger index restored. |

Tactical full-cylinder replacement proposal, 2.85 s: release 0.25, eject contents 0.65, present loader 1.15, seat/withdraw 1.60, close 2.20, ready 2.85. Some ejected rounds are live; do not depict all as spent or invent individual chamber accounting from an unspecified ammo state. Gameplay reserve accounting remains a separate implementation decision. This art proposal uses a full replacement for both states, rather than a fake box-magazine reload.

Weight: a small right-wrist dip when the left leaves the grip; sharp low-amplitude ejector stop; short inward cylinder seating check; cuff/fur lag after the frame settles. Separate loader approach, insertion, withdrawal and cylinder closure silhouettes. No camera roll, spins or floating rounds.
