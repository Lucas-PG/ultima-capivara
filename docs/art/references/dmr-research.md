Charging pull gives rearward resistance and release gives a short forward mechanical snap. Receiver moves first; wood, sling, cuffs and fur follow with diminishing lag. Keep camera shake smaller than weapon motion and avoid cumulative drift.

No reverse wrist, upward fingers, extra digits or clipping. Hidden digits remain anatomically accounted for rather than forced into view.
