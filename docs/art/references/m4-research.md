# M4 first-person research
Updated 29 September 2026. See [shared evidence](animation-research.md) and [image QA](README.md).

## Source and scope
Navy receiver/handguard, coral palm frond, brass hardware, curved magazine, cyan stock stripe and open rear notch follow weapon-m4.png. The benchmark README documents a tested 2.5-second reload and 0.22-second ADS transition. Those local values supersede the old 2.3/2.8-second research proposal. This task has not retested the runtime or changed it.

Comparison candidates: MW2019 M4A1, MWII M4, MWIII MCW, BF2042 M5A3, Valorant Phantom/Vandal, Apex R-301, Overwatch Soldier: 76, Fortnite Ballistic ARs. Transfer CoD weight and magazine continuity, Riot's readable beat structure and Apex sight clearance. Soldier's rifle and sprint ability are stylization references, not a conventional ADS or real M4 mechanism.

## Hip, ADS, sprint and inspect
Hip places rifle mainly in lower-right quarter, left paw on rearmost handguard just ahead of well, right paw on pistol grip. Both cuffs readable. ADS uses thin open notch and front post aligned at center; stock stays below sightline, 0.22 s transition per benchmark. No added scope, aperture ring or paw over rail.

Sprint proposal: 0.18 s lower/cant, 0.25 s recover; both contacts retained for ordinary running. A one-paw muzzle-up tactical sprint would be a separate unapproved animation feature. Inspect proposal: 0-.35 lift, .35-1.0 show coral flank, 1.0-1.65 reveal opposite flank, 1.65-2.1 return. Both contacts retained, index clear.

## Reload key poses
Both sequences finish at 2.50 s to match the documented benchmark. Times are proposed pose samples inside that constraint.

| Time | Tactical, chamber loaded | Empty |
| --- | --- | --- |
| .20 | Modest 20-30 degree presentation roll; left leaves handguard | Same; bolt held back |
| .65 | Withdraw partial magazine in left paw | Withdraw empty magazine, well visibly empty |
| 1.25 | Retained magazine stowed below frame, fresh magazine aligned with gap | Empty magazine offscreen, one fresh magazine aligned |
| 1.75 | Seat magazine with heel of left paw at base | Same; short upward weight response |
| 2.00 | Left moves directly toward support grip | Left index/thumb contacts left bolt catch; carrier closes |
| 2.50 | Original hip contacts restored | Original hip contacts restored |

Do not pull the rear charging handle on a chambered tactical reload. Do not move the right-side ejection port to the left to make the board easier. Right index stays out of guard until ready. The fresh magazine remains visible through alignment and seating, without teleportation.

## Weight and verification
Slight muzzle sag when support departs; one compact seating impulse, smaller carrier impulse, damped return. Hold contact extremes for 2-3 frames at 60 fps without changing total duration. Selected images remain failed references as detailed in README. Their grip positions must not override these notes.
