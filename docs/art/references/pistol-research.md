# Pistola first-person research
Updated 29 September 2026. [Shared evidence and anatomy](animation-research.md). [Image checklist](README.md).

## Design and comparison
Teal reciprocating slide, bronze frame, brass barrel details, wood palm-inlay grip. Detachable magazine through grip base. Orange front dot between two teal rear dots. Do not move rear safety/hammer hardware to act as a slide stop.

Class candidates: MW2019 M19/1911, MWII P890, MWIII COR-45, BF2042 G57, Valorant Classic/Ghost, Apex P2020, Overwatch Mercy's sidearm, Fortnite Ballistic Ranger Pistol. Their reload silhouettes help compare grip-base access and compact hand arcs. Mercy and Valorant sidearms are not conventional ADS references; use the supplied iron sights. These are comparison selections, not watched frame measurements. Primary principles are documented in [Infinity Ward's animation interview](https://blog.activision.com/call-of-duty/2019-07/Modern-Warfare-Initial-Intel-Detailing-Advancements-in-Animation-and-Authenticity), [Riot's arsenal article](https://playvalorant.com/en-gb/news/dev/how-the-valorant-arsenal-was-built/) and [Ballistic launch](https://www.fortnite.com/news/fortnite-goes-first-person-with-ballistic-a-tactical-5v5-shooter-launching-in-early-access-dec-11).

## Hip and ADS
Hip low-right with elbow extension into depth, not a side-on gun floating at center. Right paw high on backstrap; index in guard, two fingers around front strap, thumb left. Left paw cups grip/firing paw from left and below. Support thumb forward on fixed frame below slide. Keep fleshy web, fur and claws out of slide and hammer travel. At ADS, orange front dot centered in rear notch and on panel center, equal height to rear sight shoulders. Same contacts as hip.

## Sprint and inspect
Sprint proposal: .15 s dip, light two-paw carry, .20 s recover. No automatic assumption of dual wield or pistol twirl. Inspect: 0-.25 lift, .25-.85 show wood inlay, .85-1.35 opposite face, 1.35-1.75 re-cup. Keep the trigger clear and both paws connected. Never inspect by pointing muzzle back toward the face.

## Reload timeline
Times are original art targets, not reference-game statistics.

| Key | Tactical, 1.60 s | Empty, 1.95 s |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Present | .00-.15 support uncups, slide closed | .00-.15 support uncups, slide locked rearward |
| Remove | .15-.40 withdraw/stow partial mag | .15-.45 empty mag clear of grip, open well |
| Retrieve | .40-.80 fresh mag approaches with gap | .45-.85 fresh mag approaches, slide still back |
| Seat | .80-1.10 heel pushes base, slight upward response | .85-1.20 seat fully, slide remains back |
| Chamber / regrip | 1.10-1.30 re-cup only | 1.20-1.50 thumb contacts actual frame slide stop, slide closes |
| Ready | 1.30-1.60 settle | 1.50-1.95 re-cup and settle |

Board samples .15, .45, .85, 1.20, 1.50, 1.95 s. Magazine stays rigidly held through travel; no grip scaling to hide it. Tactical reload must not rack or release an already closed slide. Firing index is outside the guard in all manipulation keys.

## Weight and limitations
Magazine seating gives one small upward nudge; slide closure a quick forward snap. The wrists settle before cloth. No large camera kick. Selected reload attempt 4 corrects the forward slide-stop contact and slide sequence. Hidden thumbs, index/guard overlap and final support cup still prevent a complete anatomy pass. Attempt 5 was rejected after losing the seating paw. Use the written mechanism for blocking and the README for current image defects.
