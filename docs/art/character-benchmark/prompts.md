## Character design

Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: production character design sheet for a polished 3D multiplayer island adventure game, Ultima Capivara.
Primary request: invent a fresh, distinctive adult anthropomorphic capybara player character, with the finish of a high-end real-time game hero. This is the original character design, no weapon yet.
Character: upright, compact sturdy adult body, grounded proportions, slightly elongated barrel torso, unmistakable broad blunt rectangular capybara muzzle, small round ears, alert half-relaxed dark expressive eyes with actual eyelids, subtle cheek planes and whisker follicles. Warm chestnut short coarse fur with directional grooming, light caramel muzzle and inner forearms, dark small leathery nose. Confident easygoing personality. The hands have exactly FOUR digits each: three broad articulated fingers and one opposable thumb, short dark blunt nails, defined knuckles and palm pads. Fur extends down the backs of the hands, with leathery inner pads.
Design freedom: create an original practical tropical island explorer outfit with a strong memorable silhouette and clean readable construction. Use a cropped weathered deep blue utility vest over a warm ivory linen shirt with sleeves rolled below the elbow, muted olive tailored field trousers ending above the ankles, compact canvas belt pouches, one restrained coral fabric accent and a small team-color neck scarf. Bare capybara feet with three short toes. Fabric thickness, visible seams, stitching, convincing folds at the elbows, woven cuff fibers, restrained wear. Compact gear, never bulky military armor. Character remains clearly animal, not a human wearing an animal head.
Composition: premium landscape 3D character turnaround artboard on a neutral warm gray studio background. Large full-body three-quarter hero at left; clean front, side, and back full-body views at consistent smaller scale on right. Small head close-up and open four-digit paw detail at bottom. Consistent identical anatomy, outfit, colors and proportions across all views.
Lighting: neutral soft studio key and cool gentle fill, enough grazing light to reveal short fur and fabric, realistic material response with restrained stylization.
Constraints: no gun, no helmet, no text, no logos, no UI, no scenery, no exaggerated baby/chibi proportions, no five-finger human hands, no rubbery tubes, no floating parts. Prioritize a beautiful coherent buildable character over excessive tiny ornaments.
