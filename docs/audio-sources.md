# Game audio sources

Every sound in the game is generated by the project's own code. There are no recordings, samples or third-party sound files in the build, and nothing is downloaded at runtime.

- `src/sound/` holds the recipes: weapons (`weapons.ts`), movement, surfaces and gun handling (`foley.ts`), feedback, interface and capybara voices (`feedback.ts`), ambience beds and island life (`ambience.ts`) and music (`music.ts`), built from a small DSP kit (`dsp.ts`: filters, modal resonators, Karplus-Strong strings, noise, envelopes, echoes).
- `src/sound/bank.ts` lists every sound with its variants and sample rate, renders it and normalizes it to -20 LUFS. `src/sound/bake.worker.ts` renders the bank in a Web Worker after the first audio unlock, combat sounds first.
- `src/sound/mix.ts` is the mix: target loudness per category, the distance and occlusion models and the ambience policy. `src/audio.ts` plays the baked buffers through that mix.
- The music (bossa nova menu loop, drop samba, endgame pulse, victory and defeat stingers, the dance emote's batucada) is original, composed in code for this project.

The previous bank (CC0 recordings from The Free Firearm Sound Library, Kenney RPG Audio and BMacZero's Gun Reload Sound Effects) was removed from `public/audio/` in the 2026-09 audio pass; it is no longer used or shipped.

Budget: about 9 M float samples on High (36 MB of AudioBuffers) and 5 M on Low (Leve, mono beds and music, fewer variants), baked in about 2 s in the worker. Audition everything with `tools/audio/lab.html` on a dev server, or render WAVs and spectrograms with `npx tsx tools/audio/render-bank.ts --out <dir> --png`.
